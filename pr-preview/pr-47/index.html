      Globe mode

      Timeline shows what happened when. Globe mode shows where.

      Coloured shapes are approximate spheres of control, cultural presence, or inhabited footprints (schematic polygons), not exact ancient borders. Polities are solid, peoples are hatched with a dashed edge, and presence is a soft wash, all shown together (see the key on the globe). From 1815 the hand-drawn empires give way to real state borders (Natural Earth, regrouped by year), with colonies and vassal states shown paler in their ruler's colour; before 1914, land outside any state is left to the peoples and presence layers.
